Offbeat is a local-first festival companion for lineups, personal schedules and coordinating with friends when mobile networks become unreliable.

PLAN YOUR FESTIVAL
• Browse festival days, stages and set times
• View the lineup as a visual timeline
• Star the artists you want to see
• Spot schedule clashes before they catch you out
• See what is playing now and what comes next

STAY TOGETHER
• Create festival groups and invite friends with a QR code
• Share manual stage and meeting-point check-ins
• See the sets your group has starred
• Chat with your group and in festival channels

BUILT FOR FESTIVAL CONDITIONS
Festival information is cached on your device. Offbeat is designed to remain useful when internet access is slow, intermittent or unavailable, and to synchronise again when a route becomes available.

Availability of festival data and peer-to-peer features can vary by event, device and connection conditions.
