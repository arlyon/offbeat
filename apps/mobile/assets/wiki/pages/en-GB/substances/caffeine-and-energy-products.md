# Caffeine and energy products

> **Get emergency help now** for severe chest pain, collapse, seizure, dangerous overheating, confusion or loss of coordination, abnormal breathing, loss of consciousness, or a very fast or irregular heartbeat with serious symptoms. Follow [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help). Do not wait for caffeine to wear off or try to counter it with alcohol, sedatives or another drug.

Caffeine is a widely consumed stimulant found in coffee, tea, cola, energy drinks and some medicines. It is also sold in energy shots, tablets, powders, concentrated liquids, pre-workout products and mixed supplements. Ordinary food and drink use should not be confused with concentrated-product poisoning, but familiar branding does not make every amount, person or festival situation safe.

## Product and concentration uncertainty

Caffeine content can differ widely between products, serving sizes and batches. A container may hold several servings. Supplements may contain caffeine from several listed ingredients, including guarana, kola nut or tea extracts, and an "energy" or pre-workout product may combine caffeine with other stimulants.

Pure powder and concentrated liquid create particular measuring and mix-up risks: a small volume can contain much more caffeine than an ordinary drink. Never estimate powder with a household spoon, guess a concentrate, dry-scoop a product intended to be diluted, or assume one scoop, tablet, shot or bottle equals one standard amount. If the label, concentration, serving size or ingredients are missing or unclear, do not consume it.

Appearance, taste, a seller's description and the feeling of stimulation cannot establish caffeine concentration or rule out another ingredient. Keep the label or packaging for medical staff if someone becomes unwell.

## Heart, anxiety, heat and sleep harms

Caffeine can cause jitteriness, anxiety, restlessness, nausea, diarrhoea, difficulty concentrating, sleeplessness and palpitations. Larger or unexpectedly concentrated amounts can cause severe agitation, rapid or abnormal heart rhythms and seizures. Harm can occur at lower amounts in people who are sensitive to caffeine or have underlying conditions such as heart disease or high blood pressure.

Caffeine can make someone feel more awake, but it does not restore missed sleep, reverse impairment or make an exhausted person fit to drive, swim or navigate crowds safely. Repeated use to push through a festival can extend sleep loss and worsen anxiety, irritability, poor judgement, paranoia and later deterioration.

Heat, prolonged dancing or exertion, illness and stimulant combinations can make a fast heartbeat, agitation and overheating harder to assess. Move away from heat and exertion when symptoms begin and involve festival medical staff early. A very high temperature, fast breathing or heartbeat, confusion, loss of coordination, seizure or loss of consciousness may be heatstroke or another emergency. Do not force drinks or use a fixed hydration target, and do not give anything by mouth to someone who is confused, very drowsy or unable to swallow normally.

## Stimulant stacking and sedatives

Count all possible caffeine sources rather than only coffee or energy drinks. Combining caffeine products with cocaine, MDMA, speed, cathinones, methamphetamine, some decongestants, prescribed stimulants or another energy product can add heart, blood-pressure, agitation, seizure and overheating risk. Unknown mixed supplements make the total harder to judge. See [Dangerous drug combinations](wiki:drug-testing.dangerous-combinations).

Caffeine does not sober someone up or reverse alcohol, opioid, GHB/GBL, benzodiazepine or other sedative poisoning. It may make the situation look temporarily less obvious while dangerous impairment or abnormal breathing continues. Never give caffeine, food, drink or medicine to an unconscious or significantly impaired person.

## No universal safe festival limit

Food Standards Agency guidance for the general population cannot be turned into one safe festival limit. It does not account for every single serving, concentrated or mixed product, pregnancy, age, medicine, health condition, caffeine sensitivity, sleep loss, heat, exertion or other drug. Staying below a general daily figure is not a guarantee of safety, and feeling tolerant is not evidence that the heart, temperature or sleep effects are harmless.

Follow the product label and do not exceed it, but treat the label as a ceiling rather than a promise of safety. Avoid stacking products, increasing servings because the expected effect is absent, or using caffeine to postpone needed sleep. Ask a pharmacist or clinician about caffeine with medicines or a health condition.

## If someone is unwell

Stop further caffeine and other stimulants, reduce exertion, move to a cooler place if safe and stay with the person. Seek festival medical help early for persistent palpitations, repeated vomiting, severe anxiety or agitation, worsening tremor, inability to sleep with deteriorating mental health, or symptoms that are unexpected for the claimed product.

Severe or worsening symptoms, any emergency sign above, or suspected ingestion of a concentrated or uncertain product requires [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help). Do not make the person vomit or delay care while calculating an amount.
