# 4-AcO-DMT

> **Human safety evidence is very limited.** Published evidence includes laboratory and animal work and self-selected reports, not a strong clinical evidence base for illicit use, uncommon routes, combinations or current UK products. Important harms may be undocumented. Evidence gaps must not be read as evidence of safety.

4-AcO-DMT, also called psilacetin or O-acetylpsilocin, is a synthetic psychedelic. Chemical similarity and evidence that it can produce psilocin do not establish that its effects, potency, contaminants or risks are equivalent to psilocybin mushrooms.

A powder, capsule, edible or product sold as "synthetic mushrooms" may contain a different drug or mixture. Appearance, taste, expected effects and reagent colours cannot confirm identity, dose or purity.

## Uncertain effects and risks

Reported effects include altered perception, confusion, panic, anxiety, nausea and impaired judgement, but their frequency and dose relationship are not well established. Product mislabelling, measurement error, combinations and limited toxicology data add uncertainty.

Do not assume a physical symptom is part of an expected psychedelic experience. Do not use chemical similarity, an expected duration or an online report to predict that someone will remain medically stable.

## Supporting distress

If the person is alert, breathing normally and not in immediate danger, move to a quiet place away from roads, water, heights, fire and crowds. Have one calm sober person stay, use simple reassurance and avoid confrontation. Do not give another drug to end the experience.

If distress is escalating, the person cannot be kept safe, or there is uncertainty about physical symptoms, involve festival medical or welfare staff early.

## Combinations

There is not enough evidence to label combinations safe. Other psychedelics or cannabis may intensify confusion and panic. Stimulants may add agitation, overheating and cardiovascular strain. MAOIs and other serotonergic substances create uncertain and potentially serious interaction risks. See [Dangerous drug combinations](wiki:drug-testing.dangerous-combinations).

## Emergency signs

Immediate danger to self or others, severe or escalating agitation, collapse, seizure, severe chest pain, dangerous overheating, abnormal breathing, loss of consciousness, stroke-like signs or rapid worsening requires [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) immediately.

Do not wait for a test, an expected peak or an expected end time. Preserve packaging or a sample for responders when safe, but do not delay care to collect it.
