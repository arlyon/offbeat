# 2C-B

> **Evidence is limited.** Clinical and public-health evidence about 2C-B, especially uncommon routes, combinations and current illicit products, is much weaker than for many established drugs. Absence of documented harm is not evidence of safety.

2C-B is a synthetic psychedelic that can also produce stimulant-like physical effects. A tablet or powder sold as 2C-B may contain another drug or mixture. Products called "pink cocaine" or "tusi" often contain mixtures and should not be assumed to contain 2C-B or cocaine.

Appearance, taste, effects and a reagent colour cannot confirm identity, strength or purity. Home testing can only flag some inconsistencies. See [Professional and at-home drug checking](wiki:drug-testing.professional-and-at-home).

## Possible effects and risks

Reported effects include altered perception, anxiety, panic, confusion, nausea, raised heart rate and raised blood pressure. Product uncertainty and limited surveillance make frequency and severity hard to estimate. Dangerous agitation, overheating, seizures and cardiovascular symptoms must not be dismissed as an intense psychedelic experience.

Mental-health vulnerability, an unfamiliar environment, crowds and combining substances can increase distress or unsafe behaviour.

## Combinations

Evidence for specific 2C-B interactions is sparse. Combining it with stimulants may add cardiovascular, agitation and overheating risks. Combining psychedelics or cannabis may intensify confusion and panic. MAOIs and other serotonergic substances create uncertain and potentially serious interaction risks. No unstudied combination should be inferred safe.

See [Dangerous drug combinations](wiki:drug-testing.dangerous-combinations).

## Supporting distress

For distress without physical red flags or immediate danger, move to a quieter place away from roads, heights, water and crowds. Have one calm sober person stay, speak simply and avoid confrontation. Do not use another drug to end the experience.

If the person may harm themselves or others, cannot be kept away from danger, is severely or increasingly agitated, or loses meaningful contact with reality, get medical or security help.

## Emergency signs

Severe chest pain, collapse, seizure, dangerous overheating, abnormal breathing, loss of consciousness, stroke-like signs or severe and escalating agitation requires [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) immediately. Do not wait for an expected duration or try to identify the product from the symptoms.
