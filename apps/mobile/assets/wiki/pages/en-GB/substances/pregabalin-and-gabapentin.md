# Pregabalin and gabapentin

> **CALL 999 OR 112 NOW** if someone is unconscious, cannot be woken, has slow, shallow, irregular or stopped breathing, has blue or grey lips or skin, makes snoring or gurgling sounds, has a seizure, collapses or is rapidly getting worse. Follow [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help). Do not let them "sleep it off" or wait to identify a tablet.

Pregabalin and gabapentin are prescription gabapentinoid medicines used for conditions including nerve pain and epilepsy; pregabalin is also used for anxiety. They can cause serious harm when prescribed, borrowed, bought or used non-medically. A prescription does not make an unsafe combination safe.

## Sedation, coordination and immediate safety

Both medicines can cause sleepiness, dizziness, blurred vision, poor coordination and difficulty concentrating or making decisions. Effects can lead to falls, road injuries, drowning, burns or aspiration after vomiting. Keep a heavily impaired person away from traffic, vehicles, water, heights, fires, cooking equipment, cold and crowd pressure. Stay with them and involve festival medical staff early.

If they are unconscious but breathing normally, use the recovery position and keep checking. If they are not breathing normally, start CPR and follow the emergency call handler. Do not give food, drink, coffee or another drug, make them vomit, put them in a shower, or make them walk.

## Prescribed and non-medical use are different situations

If pregabalin or gabapentin was prescribed, follow its label and the prescriber's instructions. Do not take more, change the timing, use somebody else's supply or share it. Ask a pharmacist or prescriber before combining it with alcohol, another medicine or a recreational drug. If side effects, lost medicine or pain control are a problem, seek clinical advice rather than changing treatment at a festival.

Pregabalin and gabapentin are different medicines. Their strengths, schedules, uses and effects are not interchangeable, and a value stated for one cannot be converted into a value for the other. A blister pack, capsule colour, imprint or seller claim cannot prove that a non-prescribed product is genuine or establish its strength. See [Unknown substances and counterfeit products](wiki:drug-testing.unknown-and-counterfeit).

## Breathing danger and sedative combinations

Pregabalin and gabapentin can depress the central nervous system. The MHRA has reported severe respiratory depression with each medicine, including cases without an opioid. Risk is especially concerning when either is combined with an opioid or another sedative.

Opioids, benzodiazepines or Z-drugs, alcohol, GHB/GBL and other sedatives can combine with pregabalin or gabapentin to suppress consciousness and breathing. Ketamine can add severe impairment, vomiting and aspiration risk. A stimulant may hide sleepiness but does not protect breathing. See [Dangerous drug combinations](wiki:drug-testing.dangerous-combinations).

Inability to wake someone or abnormal breathing is an emergency even if the medicine was prescribed or the amount seemed familiar. If opioid exposure is possible and naloxone is available, give it according to its supplied instructions or your training while someone calls 999 or 112. Naloxone only reverses opioid effects; it does not reverse pregabalin, gabapentin, alcohol or other sedatives and does not replace breathing support or an ambulance.

## Dependence and dangerous abrupt withdrawal

Pregabalin and gabapentin can cause tolerance, physical dependence, addiction and withdrawal, including during prescribed use. Dependence is not the same as addiction and is not a reason for blame. Withdrawal can happen after abruptly stopping or sharply reducing the medicine and may include anxiety, agitation, difficulty sleeping, sweating, nausea, pain, tremor or seizures. Stopping a medicine used for epilepsy can also allow seizures to return.

> **Do not stop prescribed pregabalin or gabapentin abruptly.** Speak to the prescriber, a pharmacist or a drug service. A reduction plan must be individual and clinically supported; do not copy another person's schedule or use an unknown product to manage withdrawal.

A seizure, severe confusion, collapse, abnormal breathing or immediate danger requires [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help). Tell responders which medicine was prescribed, whether doses were missed or changed, and what alcohol, drugs or other medicines may be involved. Keep packaging when safe, but never delay care to find it.
