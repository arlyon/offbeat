# DMT and changa

> **Get emergency help now** for collapse, seizure, severe chest pain or headache, dangerous overheating, abnormal breathing, loss of consciousness, stroke-like signs, a very fast or irregular heartbeat, or severe agitation, panic or confusion that creates immediate danger. Follow [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) and do not wait to identify the product or for an expected trip to end.

DMT is a powerful, short-acting psychedelic when smoked or vaporised. Its effects can begin abruptly and be overwhelming even when the acute experience is brief. A powder, crystal, cartridge, smoking mixture or claimed effect cannot confirm identity, strength or contents.

## DMT, changa and ayahuasca are not interchangeable

- **DMT** here means the substance N,N-DMT, commonly encountered as a powder, crystal or vaping product.
- **Changa** is a smoking mixture that may contain DMT and plant material with monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) activity. Recipes, ingredients, strength and the type or amount of MAOI can vary or be undisclosed. A product called changa must not be treated as DMT alone.
- **Ayahuasca** is an oral brew or preparation, usually combining DMT-containing and MAOI-containing plants. It is not smoked DMT or changa. Its course, composition and interaction risks are different and may be prolonged.

Names do not prove composition. A product sold under any of these names may contain different plants, another drug or an unknown mixture.

## Psychological and environmental risks

DMT can cause profound changes in perception, loss of awareness of surroundings, confusion, panic, fear and impaired judgement. A person may suddenly fall, move unpredictably or be unable to respond normally. Flames, hot smoking equipment, roads, water, heights, vehicles and dense crowds can turn brief impairment into serious injury.

Sleep loss, an unfamiliar or chaotic setting, and a personal or family history of psychosis or bipolar disorder may increase mental-health risk. Severe anxiety, paranoia or altered perception that continues or worsens after the acute effects need clinical assessment.

If distress is mild and there are no physical red flags, move away from hazards and stimulation. Have one calm sober person stay, speak simply and avoid arguing about perceptions. Do not restrain the person unless needed to prevent immediate harm, and do not give another drug to end the experience. Involve festival welfare or medical staff early if distress is not settling.

## MAOIs, medicines and combinations

> **Treat changa and ayahuasca as possible MAOI exposure.** Their variable composition means a seller's ingredient list, previous experience or a timing rule cannot rule out a serious interaction.

MAOIs can interact dangerously with serotonergic medicines and drugs and with stimulants. Risks can include severe serotonin toxicity or a dangerous rise in blood pressure. Relevant substances may include some antidepressants, MDMA, amphetamines and other stimulants, tramadol, lithium, linezolid, dextromethorphan and some supplements, but no short list can establish that a combination is safe.

Do not stop or skip prescribed medicine in order to use DMT, changa or ayahuasca. Antidepressants can cause withdrawal and other harm if stopped suddenly. Ask a pharmacist or prescriber about interactions and describe the product honestly, including possible MAOI-containing plants. See [Dangerous drug combinations](wiki:drug-testing.dangerous-combinations).

Cannabis and other psychedelics may intensify panic, confusion and loss of contact with surroundings. Stimulants may add agitation, overheating and cardiovascular strain. Alcohol or sedatives do not cancel a psychedelic or MAOI-related emergency and can add impairment, vomiting, unconsciousness and breathing risk.

## Emergency signs

Heavy sweating, a very high temperature, worsening agitation or confusion, muscle rigidity, tremor, diarrhoea, severe headache, chest pain, an abnormal heartbeat or seizure may be serotonin toxicity, heat illness, a severe blood-pressure reaction or another poisoning. Do not try to diagnose which one before seeking help.

Immediate danger to self or others, inability to keep the person away from hazards, rapid worsening or any physical red flag requires [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help). Keep packaging or a sample for responders when safe, but do not delay care to collect or test it.
