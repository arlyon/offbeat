# Psilocybin mushrooms

Psilocybin mushrooms are psychedelics whose potency varies by species, specimen, growing conditions, storage and preparation. Appearance, a photograph, taste or effects cannot confirm species, identity or strength.

> **An unknown or wild mushroom is a possible poisoning.** Some poisonous species can be mistaken for psilocybin mushrooms, and serious organ damage may begin with delayed or apparently mild symptoms.

## Possible poisoning

Do not wait for psychedelic effects to pass if the mushroom was wild, foraged or uncertain. Contact urgent medical services for suspected mushroom poisoning even if the person initially seems well. Severe abdominal pain, repeated vomiting or diarrhoea, collapse, seizure, confusion, unusual drowsiness, breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness or rapid worsening requires [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) immediately.

Do not make the person vomit or give a home remedy. Keep remaining mushrooms, packaging and clear photographs for clinicians when this can be done safely. Store the sample away from food and other people and wash hands after handling. Do not rely on an app, forum or image match to rule out a toxic species.

## Psychedelic effects and distress

Psilocybin can alter perception, time, mood and judgement. Panic, confusion, paranoia, nausea and unsafe behaviour can occur. Roads, water, heights, traffic, fire and crowds add risk. Mental-health vulnerability and combining drugs may increase distress.

For mild distress without physical red flags or immediate danger, move to a quieter place away from hazards. Have one calm sober person stay, reduce stimulation and reassure them simply. Do not argue about perceptions or give another drug to end the experience.

Immediate danger, severe or escalating agitation, collapse, seizure, chest pain, dangerous overheating, abnormal breathing or loss of consciousness requires [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help).

## Combinations

Cannabis and other psychedelics can intensify confusion and panic. Stimulants may add agitation and cardiovascular strain. MAOIs can create unpredictable and potentially serious interactions. See [Dangerous drug combinations](wiki:drug-testing.dangerous-combinations).
