# MDMA

MDMA is a stimulant and serotonergic drug commonly sold as tablets, crystals or powder. A logo, colour or appearance cannot establish contents. Tablets with the same design may differ, and products sold as MDMA may contain other stimulants or unexpected drugs.

## Main risks

MDMA can raise heart rate and body temperature and alter mood, perception and judgement. Crowds, hot weather and prolonged exertion increase overheating risk. Severe toxicity can involve heatstroke, abnormal heart rhythm, agitation, confusion, seizure, organ damage or collapse.

Both too little and too much fluid can be dangerous. Excess water can lower blood sodium, causing headache, vomiting, confusion, seizure or coma. There is no universal fluid target that is safe for every person, temperature, activity level or health condition.

## Medicines, supplements and combinations

Combining MDMA with cocaine, amphetamines or other stimulants increases cardiovascular and overheating risk. MAOIs and other serotonergic medicines or drugs can cause life-threatening toxicity. Interactions can involve some antidepressants, tramadol, lithium, linezolid, dextromethorphan and supplements, but a short list cannot predict every interaction.

Do not stop prescribed medicine abruptly in order to take MDMA. Ask a pharmacist or prescriber about interactions. Do not use 5-HTP as neuroprotection or recovery treatment. Evidence does not show that it prevents harm, and it can add serotonergic risk. See [Dangerous drug combinations](wiki:drug-testing.dangerous-combinations).

## Reduce immediate risk

The lowest-risk option is not to use. Drug checking cannot prove identity, purity, dose or safety. Avoid mixing drugs. Take breaks from heat and exertion and seek welfare or medical help early if someone feels unwell.

Avoid both dehydration and forced or excessive water intake. No fixed drinking schedule can account for all circumstances. Do not use salt, supplements or sports drinks to treat confusion, vomiting or suspected low sodium without clinical assessment.

## Emergency signs

Dangerous heat, confusion, seizure, muscle rigidity, collapse, severe chest pain, worsening agitation, difficulty breathing, repeated vomiting with worsening headache, or inability to wake someone requires [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) immediately.

Move away from heat only if this can be done safely, but do not delay the emergency response while trying to cool, hydrate, test the product or decide whether symptoms fit MDMA. Do not give drinks to a confused, very drowsy or unconscious person.

## Recovery and support

Low mood, anxiety, poor sleep and fatigue can follow use, but they do not prove serotonin injury or identify what was taken. Seek clinical help for severe, persistent or worsening physical or mental-health symptoms. Rest and ordinary food and fluids may support general recovery when the person is fully alert, but no supplement has been shown to provide neuroprotection after MDMA.
