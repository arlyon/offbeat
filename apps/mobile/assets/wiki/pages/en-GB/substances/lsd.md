# LSD

LSD is a psychedelic commonly sold on blotter, as small tablets or as liquid. A blotter design, taste, claimed amount or effect cannot confirm identity or strength. Other potent substances can be sold on blotter, and products from the same sheet or design may differ.

## Main risks

Effects can be prolonged and may include altered perception, confusion, panic, paranoia and impaired judgement. Roads, traffic, water, heights, fire and crowds can become dangerous. A distressed person may misread reassurance or act unpredictably.

Sleep deprivation, an unfamiliar or chaotic setting, and a personal or family history of psychosis or bipolar disorder may increase mental-health risk. Persistent symptoms after the acute experience need clinical assessment.

## Supporting distress

If the person is alert, breathing normally and not in immediate danger, move to a quieter, lower-stimulation place away from hazards. Have one calm sober person stay. Speak simply, remind them that they are not alone and avoid arguing about perceptions.

Do not restrain them unless needed to prevent immediate harm. Do not give alcohol, cannabis, sedatives or another drug to try to end the experience. Involve festival welfare or medical staff early if distress is not settling.

## Combinations

Cannabis and other psychedelics can intensify anxiety, confusion and perceptual effects. Stimulants may add agitation, overheating and cardiovascular strain. Evidence for many combinations is incomplete, so lack of a known interaction does not establish safety. See [Dangerous drug combinations](wiki:drug-testing.dangerous-combinations).

## Emergency signs

Immediate danger to self or others, severe or escalating agitation, collapse, seizure, severe chest pain, dangerous overheating, abnormal breathing, loss of consciousness or stroke-like signs requires [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) immediately.

Do not assume physical symptoms are anxiety, wait for a trip to end or use an expected duration to delay care. If the product is uncertain, preserve packaging or a sample for responders when safe.

## Product checking

A reagent reaction cannot confirm LSD or rule out other substances. Never infer identity from a blotter image, taste or effects. An unexpected or uncertain result is a reason not to use the material and to seek professional analysis where available. See [Professional and at-home drug checking](wiki:drug-testing.professional-and-at-home).
