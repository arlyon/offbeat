# Dangerous drug combinations

> **Take extra care when combining substances.** Effects can compound or interact unpredictably. Contents, strength, timing, health conditions and individual response are often uncertain, and undeclared drugs can cause an interaction even when mixing was not intended.

If someone is unconscious, cannot be woken, is not breathing normally, has a seizure, collapses, has severe chest pain, is dangerously hot, has stroke-like signs or is rapidly worsening, follow [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) now.

## Depressant stacking

Alcohol, [opioids](wiki:substances.opioids-and-potent-synthetic-opioids), [GHB or GBL](wiki:substances.ghb-gbl), [benzodiazepines or sleeping tablets](wiki:substances.benzodiazepines-and-street-benzos), and other sedatives can combine to suppress breathing and consciousness. [Pregabalin and gabapentin](wiki:substances.pregabalin-and-gabapentin) can add sedation and breathing risk, particularly with opioids or other depressants. [Ketamine](wiki:substances.ketamine) can add severe impairment, vomiting, injury and aspiration risk. The person may become unrousable with little warning.

Alcohol with GHB or GBL is particularly unpredictable because GHB/GBL concentration varies and both depress consciousness and breathing. Alcohol with ketamine increases loss of coordination, unconsciousness and aspiration risk.

Do not leave an impaired person alone or assume they can sleep it off. Abnormal breathing or inability to wake them is an emergency, not ordinary intoxication.

## Alcohol and cocaine

Using [alcohol](wiki:substances.alcohol) with [cocaine](wiki:substances.cocaine) adds strain to the heart and can form cocaethylene in the body. This may extend cardiovascular toxicity. Feeling less drunk does not mean alcohol impairment or poisoning has been reversed.

Chest pain, collapse, seizure, severe agitation or stroke-like signs require [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) immediately.

## Poppers and erection medicines

Combining [poppers or alkyl nitrites](wiki:substances.poppers-alkyl-nitrites) with sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil or another PDE5 inhibitor can cause a profound, potentially life-threatening fall in blood pressure, fainting and collapse. A timing rule cannot declare the combination safe because products, doses and individual clearance vary.

## Stimulant stacking

Combining [cocaine](wiki:substances.cocaine), [MDMA](wiki:substances.mdma), [speed, cathinones, methamphetamine](wiki:substances.other-stimulants) or other stimulants can increase heart rate, blood pressure, agitation, overheating, seizures and cardiovascular strain. [Caffeine and energy products](wiki:substances.caffeine-and-energy-products), some decongestants and prescribed stimulants may add to these effects.

Do not use apparent tolerance or expected duration to judge that danger has passed. Hot environments, prolonged exertion, dehydration and an unknown product increase uncertainty.

## Serotonergic and MAOI risks

MDMA and some other drugs affect serotonin. Combining serotonergic substances with MAOIs, certain antidepressants, [tramadol](wiki:substances.codeine-and-tramadol), linezolid, lithium, dextromethorphan or some supplements can cause serious toxicity. [Changa and ayahuasca](wiki:substances.dmt-and-changa) may contain variable MAOI-active ingredients. Medicine interactions are complex and cannot be reduced to a universal list.

Confusion, worsening agitation, heavy sweating, high temperature, muscle rigidity, tremor, diarrhoea or seizure may indicate serotonin syndrome or another emergency. Follow [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) rather than trying to diagnose the cause.

Do not stop a prescribed medicine abruptly to take a recreational drug. Ask a pharmacist or prescriber about interactions without concealing what may be used. Do not use 5-HTP as neuroprotection. Evidence does not establish that it prevents MDMA harm, and it is serotonergic.

## Psychedelics, cannabis and distress

[LSD](wiki:substances.lsd), [psilocybin mushrooms](wiki:substances.psilocybin-mushrooms), [2C-B](wiki:substances.2c-b), [4-AcO-DMT](wiki:substances.4-aco-dmt) and [cannabis](wiki:substances.cannabis) can intensify anxiety, confusion, paranoia and impaired judgement. Combining them or adding stimulants may make distress and physical risk harder to assess.

For mild distress without medical red flags, move away from crowds, noise, traffic, heights and water. Have one calm sober person stay with them and use simple reassurance. Do not restrain them unless needed to prevent immediate harm.

Immediate danger, severe or escalating agitation, loss of contact with reality that creates danger, collapse, seizure, chest pain, dangerous heat or abnormal breathing requires [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help).

## Unknown mixtures

A pill, powder, liquid or edible may not contain what was claimed. Reagent colours, strip results, appearance and effects cannot rule out combinations or identify all components. Treat an unexpected effect as possible poisoning, not evidence of a particular drug. See [Unknown substances and counterfeit products](wiki:drug-testing.unknown-and-counterfeit).
