# Novel and unknown psychoactive substances

> **Act on symptoms, not the name.** Call 999 or 112 now if someone is unconscious, cannot be woken, is not breathing normally, has a seizure, collapses, has severe chest pain, is dangerously hot, has stroke-like signs, is an immediate danger to themselves or others, or is rapidly worsening. Follow [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) and do not wait to identify or test the substance.

## NPS is not one drug class

“New psychoactive substances” or NPS is an umbrella label, not a chemical identity. It spans unrelated novel benzodiazepines and other sedatives, cathinones and other stimulants, synthetic opioids, synthetic cannabinoids, dissociatives and psychedelics. Products may also contain established drugs, several classes or unexpected contaminants instead of the claimed compound.

These substances can have different or opposing effects and risks. There is no class-wide NPS composition, dose, effects profile, duration, redosing interval or interaction chart. Information about one named compound, analysed sample or previous experience must not be transferred to another product sold under the same label.

“Legal high”, “research chemical”, “designer drug”, “not for human consumption”, a chemical-looking name, branding, packaging, price, seller label, claimed laboratory report or QR code does not establish identity, strength, purity, legal status or safety. A similar appearance or effect does not establish it either. Sellers may reuse names for different products, and the same substance may be sold under several names.

## Legal does not mean identified or safe

The term “legal high” is misleading. The Psychoactive Substances Act creates UK offences involving production, supply, possession with intent to supply and import or export of covered psychoactive substances; many individual drugs are separately controlled under the Misuse of Drugs Act. The exact position depends on the substance and circumstances and can change.

A seller's statement about legality is therefore not proof of what is present and is not safety information. Do not use a claimed legal status to infer quality, pharmacology or lower risk.

## Use symptom-based routes

An unknown product can produce overlapping or changing signs. Do not choose a response by guessing which class it resembles:

- **Slow, abnormal or stopped breathing; blue or grey lips or skin; gurgling; limpness; or inability to wake:** follow [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) immediately. If opioid exposure is possible and naloxone is available, give it according to its instructions or your training while help is called. Naloxone does not replace breathing support or an ambulance and will not treat non-opioid effects.
- **Severe chest pain, collapse, seizure, a very fast or irregular heartbeat, dangerous heat, stroke-like signs or severe escalating agitation:** follow [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) immediately.
- **Extreme drowsiness, repeated vomiting, confusion, poor coordination, paranoia, hallucinations or panic:** stay with the person, move away from traffic, water, heights, heat and crowds, and involve festival medical or welfare staff early. Escalate immediately if breathing is abnormal, they cannot stay awake, there is immediate danger or symptoms worsen.

Do not give another drug to counter the effects, make the person vomit, put them in a shower or let them “sleep it off” alone. Tell responders every drug, medicine and product that might be involved, without delaying care. [Dangerous drug combinations](wiki:drug-testing.dangerous-combinations) explains overlapping depressant, stimulant and serotonergic emergency patterns.

## Testing cannot make an unknown product known-safe

A reagent colour or target-specific strip can reveal only limited red flags. It cannot screen for every NPS, identify every component, measure strength or dose, rule out another drug, or prove safety. A result suggesting one substance does not show that other classes are absent, and a negative result applies only to the targets, tested portion and conditions of that method.

Professional analysis can identify more components, but a newly emerging compound may not be covered by the method or reference library. Results apply to the submitted portion; another portion, packet or batch may differ. WEDINOS requires surrender and reports later, so it is not emergency triage or an immediate festival service. See [Professional and at-home drug checking](wiki:drug-testing.professional-and-at-home).

Never taste, sniff or take a small amount to identify a product. Keep unknown material sealed and separate from medicines, food, drink and known substances. If safe, retain packaging, photographs and a sample for responders or an eligible professional service. See [Unknown substances and counterfeit products](wiki:drug-testing.unknown-and-counterfeit) for handling, counterfeit-medicine, vape and possible synthetic-opioid guidance.

## Related guides are not identification tools

If reliable professional analysis identifies a substance, use the relevant guide for that substance or class. Do not select a guide from the seller's label or observed effects alone. Separate guides cover [benzodiazepines and street benzos](wiki:substances.benzodiazepines-and-street-benzos), [cathinones and other stimulants](wiki:substances.other-stimulants), [opioids and potent synthetic opioids](wiki:substances.opioids-and-potent-synthetic-opioids) and [synthetic cannabinoids, Spice and SCRAs](wiki:substances.synthetic-cannabinoids-and-spice).

Tusi, Tuci and “pink cocaine” are specific market labels for unknown mixtures and are covered separately in [Tusi and pink cocaine](wiki:substances.tusi-pink-cocaine). No recipe or profile from that guide should be generalised to NPS.

## No imported reference data

This page deliberately has no imported dose or duration record. Attaching one compound's data would falsely imply that NPS has a stable identity, composition or expected course. No amount, timer, seller claim, test result or previous experience can show that an unknown product is safe, justify redosing, end supervision or delay medical help.
