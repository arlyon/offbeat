# Professional and at-home drug checking

> **A drug test is a red-flag tool, not a green light.** No result can prove identity, purity, strength, dose, absence of contaminants or safety. It applies only to the portion tested and only to substances that the method can detect.

## Professional checking in the UK

Professional services may use laboratory methods that separate and identify mixture components. Some methods can measure strength. The report still needs to be read for its method, detection limits and the sample analysed.

Availability and service models vary. At many UK festivals, licensed testing is back-of-house: surrendered or confiscated material informs public alerts, but the sample is not returned and the person does not receive a tailored result. Check the event's official medical, welfare and drug-alert information rather than assuming there is a walk-up service.

WEDINOS provides anonymous laboratory analysis under its current eligibility and submission rules. It requires full surrender and posts results later, so it is not an emergency or immediate festival service. Its eligibility wording and arrangements can change. Read the current WEDINOS instructions before sending anything.

## At-home reagent colour tests

A reagent colour is a low-selectivity, presumptive observation. Different substances can share colours, mixtures can mask reactions, and lighting, timing, sample size, storage and human colour judgement can alter the result.

- A chart-matching reaction means only that the tested speck reacted consistently with that chart.
- An unexpected colour or no reaction does not identify what is present.
- Several reagents provide more observations, but still do not confirm identity.
- Reagents do not measure purity, strength or dose.

Never infer identity from appearance, effects or a reagent result. Use the current instructions, chart, expiry information and safety data for the exact kit. Reagents can be corrosive. Do not copy drop counts, timing, storage or disposal instructions from another product.

## Fentanyl and nitazene strips

These strips are target-specific immunoassays, not general drug checks. Different products recognise different compounds and use different instructions.

- A negative means the target was not detected by that strip in that portion under those conditions. It does not mean opioid-free or safe.
- A positive may reflect the target or a cross-reacting substance. It does not show amount or distribution.
- New analogues, low concentrations, uneven mixing, interference, dilution, incomplete dissolution, an expired strip or procedure errors can cause misleading results.
- Fentanyl and nitazene strips are different tests. A negative result from one does not rule out the other class.

UK evidence for nitazene strips is developing. Government guidance warns that marketed strips may miss variants they were not validated for. Fentanyl-strip evidence is largely product-specific and North American, so its performance and drug-market assumptions cannot be transferred confidently to the UK.

Follow the current manufacturer's insert and local public-health guidance for the exact product and lot. Some competitive strips use line meanings that are the reverse of familiar home tests. An invalid control is no result.

## The sampling problem

A scraping is not automatically representative of a tablet, bag, wrap or batch. Contamination and active ingredients may be uneven. One negative portion can miss a localised contaminant, while one positive portion cannot show how the substance is distributed elsewhere.

Crushing, mixing or testing a larger amount cannot guarantee uniformity and may introduce handling risks. Do not publish or follow a universal preparation recipe. Use only instructions validated for the exact test and sample type.

## Handling and disposal

- Do not taste, sniff or deliberately touch an unknown substance to identify it.
- Keep powders and liquids sealed, labelled and away from faces, food, drink, children and other people.
- Avoid spreading dust. Wash hands with soap and water after accidental contact and avoid touching eyes, nose or mouth.
- Store and dispose of reagents, strips and tested material exactly as the supplier and local service instruct. Do not generalise WEDINOS postal instructions to another service.

## After a result

If a result is unexpected, invalid or conflicts with an official alert, the safest option is not to use the material. Keep it separate and seek professional analysis if a current lawful service accepts it. Do not reinterpret it through more guessing, taste, appearance or effects.

Symptoms always override a test result. If someone is unconscious, cannot be woken, is not breathing normally, has a seizure, collapses, has severe chest pain, is dangerously hot or is rapidly worsening, follow [Get emergency help](wiki:emergency.get-help) now. Do not wait to test or identify the substance.
