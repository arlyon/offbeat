# Get emergency help

> **CALL 999 OR 112 NOW** if someone is unconscious, cannot be woken, is not breathing normally, has a seizure, collapses, has severe chest pain, is dangerously hot or is rapidly getting worse.

If there is no phone signal, send someone to a steward, security team or festival medical point. Do not leave the person alone.

## While help is coming

1. Make sure you are not entering danger yourself.
2. Tell the call handler where you are, what happened and what may have been taken. Be honest about drugs, alcohol and medicines.
3. If they are unresponsive and not breathing normally, start CPR and use an AED if one is available. Follow the call handler's instructions.
4. If they are unconscious but breathing normally, place them in the recovery position and keep checking their breathing.
5. If they are having a seizure, protect them from injury, time it, do not restrain them and put nothing in their mouth.
6. Keep packaging, containers or a sample for medical staff when it is safe to do so.

## Do not

- Do not make them vomit.
- Do not give food, drink, coffee or medication to an unconscious or impaired person.
- Do not put them in a shower or bath.
- Do not let them "sleep it off" without continuous supervision.
- Do not wait to identify the substance before getting help.

This guide cannot diagnose poisoning or replace festival medics, ambulance call handlers or clinicians.
