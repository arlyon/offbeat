# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Festival attendees coordinating personal plans and friend groups on phones in crowded, noisy environments with unreliable or absent connectivity. They need fast, one-handed access in bright daylight and dark venues, without stopping to manage sync.

## Product Purpose

OFFBEAT is a local-first festival companion for browsing signed lineups, building a personal schedule, seeing friends' plans and presence, and communicating across internet, peer-to-peer, Bluetooth, and constrained mesh routes. Success means the useful product remains available offline and converges without users thinking about transports.

## Brand Personality

Direct, electric, resilient. Copy is concise and operational, with enough personality to feel native to live music rather than like enterprise software.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic rounded consumer event app, a glossy gradient-heavy nightlife promotion, or a connectivity-dependent social feed. Avoid ornamental cards, ambiguous gestures, tiny controls, and decorative motion that competes with urgent schedule information.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next useful decision ahead of navigation or decoration.
2. Make offline and partial states honest without turning transport mechanics into the task.
3. Keep personal and group privacy boundaries explicit in behavior and copy.
4. Use dense information only when hierarchy and tap targets remain clear at a glance.
5. Make shared activity visible at the item where it changes a decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain at least 44px tap targets, strong contrast against the dark palette, readable type under outdoor glare, and non-color indicators for state. Respect reduced-motion settings, preserve semantic labels for assistive technology, and avoid requiring precision gestures.
